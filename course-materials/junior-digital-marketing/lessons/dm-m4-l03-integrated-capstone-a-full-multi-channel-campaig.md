## Why Integrated Campaigns Matter

Real businesses rarely succeed with a single channel. A customer might see an Instagram ad, read a blog post, find you again through Google, and finally convert after an email. An **integrated multi-channel campaign** coordinates ads, organic content, SEO, and email so they reinforce one another around a single goal. This capstone proves you can think like a marketer, not just operate one tool.

## Step 1: Start With One Business Goal

Everything begins with a single, measurable objective. Use the SMART framework:

- **Specific**: Generate newsletter sign-ups for a new product launch
- **Measurable**: 500 new subscribers
- **Achievable**: based on current traffic and budget
- **Relevant**: tied to revenue or growth
- **Time-bound**: within 4 weeks

Write this as one sentence: *"Acquire 500 new email subscribers for the spring collection launch in 4 weeks on a $400 ad budget."* Every channel decision must serve this goal.

## Step 2: Map the Customer Journey

Think in stages and assign channels to each:

| Stage | Goal | Channels |
|-------|------|----------|
| Awareness | Reach new people | Paid social ads, organic posts |
| Consideration | Build interest/trust | Blog content, SEO landing page |
| Conversion | Capture the lead | Lead-gen form, opt-in offer |
| Retention | Nurture & sell | Email welcome series |

Notice how channels hand off to each other. An ad drives traffic to an SEO-optimized landing page; the page offers a lead magnet; the email series nurtures the subscriber.

## Step 3: Create a Consistent Message & Creative

Define one **core message** and one **visual identity** that appears everywhere:

- Same value proposition headline across ad, landing page, and email
- Same colors, fonts, and hero image
- Same call-to-action verb ("Get early access")

Consistency builds recognition and trust as users move between channels.

## Step 4: Build the Campaign Calendar

Sequence activity over the 4 weeks:

- **Week 1**: Publish SEO blog post + landing page; warm-up organic posts
- **Week 2**: Launch paid social ads driving to landing page
- **Week 3**: Retarget engagers; send first email to new subscribers
- **Week 4**: Final push ads + last-chance email; measure

## Step 5: Track Everything With UTMs

UTM parameters tell you which channel drove each result in Google Analytics.

`https://site.com/spring?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=spring_launch`

- **utm_source** = where (instagram, google, newsletter)
- **utm_medium** = type (cpc, organic, email)
- **utm_campaign** = which campaign (spring_launch)

Use a consistent naming convention so reports stay clean.

## Step 6: Measure With Channel KPIs

- **Ads**: CTR, CPC, cost per acquisition (CPA)
- **Content/SEO**: organic sessions, time on page, keyword ranking
- **Email**: open rate, click rate, conversion rate
- **Overall**: total subscribers vs. 500 goal, blended CPA

## Step 7: Package as a Portfolio Case Study

Document the campaign as a story: the goal, your strategy, the channel plan, screenshots of assets, the results (or projected results), and what you learned. This case study is the centerpiece of your job application — it shows end-to-end thinking.
