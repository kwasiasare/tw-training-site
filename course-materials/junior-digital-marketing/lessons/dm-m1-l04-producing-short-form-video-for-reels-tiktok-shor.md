## Why Short-Form Video Matters

Short-form vertical video (typically 9:16, 15-90 seconds) is the highest-reach content format on social media today. Platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts actively push this format because it keeps users on the app longer. For a junior social media assistant, knowing how to produce these videos quickly and consistently is one of the most valuable skills you can offer.

Key reasons short-form works:

- **Algorithmic reach**: Platforms show Reels/TikToks/Shorts to non-followers, so even small accounts can go viral.
- **Low production cost**: A phone is enough — no expensive gear required.
- **High engagement**: Quick, entertaining content drives comments, shares, and saves.

## The Most Important Metric: Watch Time & Retention

The algorithm rewards videos that keep people watching. Two metrics matter most:

- **Average watch time / completion rate** — how much of the video people watch.
- **Re-watches and shares** — strong signals of quality.

Everything in your video should serve one goal: keep the viewer watching to the end.

## The Hook (First 3 Seconds)

The hook is the single most important part of any short-form video. If you don't grab attention immediately, viewers swipe away.

Types of strong hooks:

- **Question hook**: "Why is no one talking about this?"
- **Bold statement**: "You're using this app wrong."
- **Visual hook**: Show the most surprising/satisfying moment first.
- **Curiosity gap**: "Wait until you see what happens at the end."
- **Relatable callout**: "If you're a small business owner, stop scrolling."

Pair a verbal hook with an on-screen text hook so it works even with the sound off.

## Trending Formats

You rarely need to invent something new. Adapt proven formats to your brand:

- **Trending audio**: Use sounds that are currently rising. On TikTok and Reels, trending audio boosts reach.
- **Listicles**: "3 tools every marketer needs."
- **Before/after**: Transformation reveals.
- **POV / storytelling**: Put the viewer in a scenario.
- **Tutorials / how-to**: Quick value-packed steps.
- **Talking head**: You or a presenter speaking directly to camera with captions.

## Planning Your Video

Before filming, write a simple plan:

1. **Goal**: What action or feeling do you want? (Follow, save, visit link)
2. **Hook**: Your first line and first frame.
3. **Shot list**: Bullet each clip you need.
4. **Script / talking points**: Keep it tight — short-form has no room for waffle.
5. **Call to action (CTA)**: "Follow for more," "Comment your question."

Example shot list:

```
Hook: close-up, "Stop wasting money on ads"
Shot 1: screen recording of analytics
Shot 2: talking head explaining tip
Shot 3: result / before-after
CTA: "Follow for daily tips"
```

## Filming on a Phone

You don't need a studio. Follow these basics:

- **Orientation**: Always film vertical (9:16).
- **Lighting**: Face a window or use a ring light. Avoid backlighting.
- **Framing**: Keep your subject centred, leave room for captions top and bottom.
- **Stability**: Use a tripod or prop your phone up.
- **Audio**: Film in a quiet room; a cheap clip-on mic improves clarity hugely.
- **Shoot extra**: Capture a few takes and some b-roll for editing flexibility.

## Editing with Mobile Tools

Free apps make editing fast: **CapCut**, **InShot**, or the native in-app editors in TikTok and Instagram.

Editing checklist:

- **Cut tight**: Remove pauses and dead air to keep pace fast.
- **Add captions**: Use auto-captions — most viewers watch on mute. Always check for errors.
- **On-screen text**: Reinforce key points and the hook.
- **Trending audio**: Add it inside the native app so the platform recognises it.
- **Pacing**: Change shots/visuals every few seconds to hold attention.
- **Keep it short**: Trim anything that doesn't add value.

## Posting Best Practices

- **Caption**: Short, with a hook or question to drive comments.
- **Hashtags**: Mix a few relevant niche and broad tags (3-5 is plenty).
- **Cover/thumbnail**: Choose a clear frame with text for the grid.
- **Timing**: Post when your audience is active; check insights.
- **Native upload**: Post directly in-app; avoid visible watermarks from other apps (e.g. TikTok logo on Reels can reduce reach).

## Workflow Summary

Plan → Hook → Film vertical → Edit tight with captions → Add trending audio → Post natively with a strong caption and CTA → Review analytics → Repeat what works.
